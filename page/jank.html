Connect to a jank: by USB (Chrome or Edge on a computer), from an iPhone MIDI browser app, or by Bluetooth (Chrome).

Connect

Bluetooth: press the jank's button three times (its LED blinks blue for two minutes), then connect. On an iPhone, open this page in the MIDIWeb Browser or Web MIDI Browser app, connect the jank in the app's Bluetooth MIDI settings, turn SysEx on there, then press MIDI port.
