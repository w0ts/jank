Player

The file plays on this computer (monitor) and on the stick's USB audio ("jank"). The first time, choose the stick's output once in the browser's own output chooser (no microphone is used); it is remembered. The stick gets the file at its true level: the volume knob and the monitor box only change what you hear here. Space plays / pauses while the pointer is over this card. After a reload the remembered folder needs one click (Play or Open) to be allowed again; on the https address Chrome 122 and later offers "Allow on every visit" in that prompt, which makes this step silent.
