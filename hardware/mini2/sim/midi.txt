faults (V5M 4.97 V, the resistors -1 %):
  jack: ring / DIN 4 shorted to GND (a mono plug, a broken cable): 21.4 mA, 53 mW in each of its 2 0805s (125 mW parts)
  DIN: ring / DIN 4 shorted to GND (a mono plug, a broken cable): 21.4 mA, 53 mW in each of its 2 0805s (125 mW parts)
  jack: tip shorted to ring with the FET on: 10.7 mA (13 mW in each of the sink's 2 0805s)
  tip shorted to GND: no current (the sink side is already at GND when the FET is on; off, nothing drives it)

SUMMARY: a receiver gets 3.37 .. 5.54 mA over the corners (MIDI 1.0's 5 V circuit aims at ~5 mA); the GPIOs drive only the FETs' gates
