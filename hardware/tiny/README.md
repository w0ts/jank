# Jank tiny: a small board anyone orders assembled from JLCPCB

Part of the **[Jank device](../../README.md)** · the study behind it: [docs/dongle.md](../../docs/dongle.md) · the
bigger board: [hardware/pcb](../pcb/README.md)

One board, two uses. **With a computer on USB-C it is the Jank stick** (network adapter, USB-MIDI, the drive).
**Alone, plugged into a piece of gear's MIDI OUT or THRU, it runs from that output's few milliamps** and sends jank's
MIDI clock into the gear's MIDI IN. An ESP32-S3-MINI-1-N8 module, a USB-C on the top edge, a 3.5 mm TRS jack on each side
(left: MIDI OUT, right: POWER IN, both via TRS-to-DIN type A adaptors), one button, one LED, a 4.4 mF reservoir and a
60 nA buck. Everything is generated by the scripts here, from one parts table (`parts.py`).

> **Stage: design for review (2026-11-10).** Nothing is ordered, built or measured. Every figure marked *unverified*
> comes from a drawing, a datasheet or an estimate, not from a part in hand. Prices and stock are one snapshot
> (`fab/parts-snapshot.json`, read 2026-11-10 from JLCPCB's parts search), not a quote.

## Schematic

```
 J3 POWER IN (gear's MIDI OUT/THRU)            USB-C J1
   ring (DIN 4) -- R1 47R -- D1 B5819W --+       VBUS -- D3 B5819W -- R5 4.7R --+
   tip  (DIN 5) -- R2 47R -- D2 B5819W --+-- VSYS (C1 + C2: 2 x 2200 uF, C3 10 uF)
   sleeve (DIN 2) = GND                    |      CC1, CC2: 5.1k to GND (R3, R4); D+/D- to GPIO20/19
                                           +-- U2 TPS62842 VIN; R7 51k = 3.3 V; L1 2.2 uH; C5 22 uF -> +3V3
                                           +-- U3 BL8506-40 (4.0 V detector) -- D4 A1 --+
                         PWR_HOLD (GPIO4) ---------------------------- D4 A2 --+-- BUCK_EN (C4 100n, R6 10M to GND)
                         VBUS ---------------------------------------- D5 --- +
                                           +-- R10 1M -- VBANK_ADC (GPIO1, C8 100n) -- R11 1M -- GND
 J2 MIDI OUT (gear's MIDI IN)                 VBUS -- R12 100k -- VBUS_SENSE (GPIO2) -- R13 100k -- GND
   ring (DIN 4) -- R14 33R -- +3V3
   tip  (DIN 5) -- R15 10R -- MIDI_TX (GPIO21)
   sleeve (DIN 2) = GND                        +3V3 -- R8 10k -- EN, C7 1u;  GPIO0 -- SW1 -- GND;  GPIO38 -- R9 5.1k -- LED1 -- GND
```

### Power from a MIDI OUT

- **Both data pins feed the reservoir:** the ring (DIN pin 4, the sender's source through its 220 ohm) and the tip (DIN
  pin 5, high between bytes), each through **47 ohm** and a **B5819W** Schottky. Nothing flows back out: each diode
  blocks when its pin is below VSYS (the tip during a 0 bit, any pin when USB holds VSYS up), leaving only the diode's
  leakage (microamps, *unverified* at these voltages).
- **The 47 ohm current limit, the trade-off:** it lowers the current from a normal 5 V / 220 ohm output only a little
  at the operating point (bank at 4.0 V, Schottky 0.25 V: (5 - 0.25 - 4.0) / 267 = **2.8 mA a pin instead of 3.4 mA**,
  -18 %; the running equilibrium with ~2.3 mA drawn moves from ~4.50 V to ~4.46 V), but it guards weak drivers when the
  bank is empty: into a flat bank a 5 V / 220 ohm output gives 17.6 mA instead of 21 mA, a **3.3 V / 33 ohm output
  (CA-033) 38 mA instead of 92 mA**. Such 3.3 V gear cannot run the dongle anyway (the bank never reaches the start
  threshold), but it is not stressed either. 47 ohm is a JLC Basic part.
- **USB joins the same rail** through D3 (B5819W) and **R5 4.7 ohm**: USB wins when present (4.7 V against the bank's
  ~4.4 V). R5 limits the inrush into 4.4 mF to ~1 A (time constant ~21 ms) instead of an unlimited surge; in stick
  mode it drops ~0.47 V at 100 mA (47 mW). *Unverified:* the 0805's pulse rating for ~49 mJ per plug-in, and that every
  host tolerates the 1 A peak (USB 2.0 allows 10 uF at attach). A 1206 (Extended) is the safer choice if it is a
  concern.

### The reservoir (C1, C2: 2 x 2200 uF 6.3 V aluminium, 10 x 10.5 mm, **4.4 mF**)

- **The start:** the module may only start once the bank holds a whole boot: the detector releases the buck at
  **~4.16 V** (BL8506-40: 4.00 V falling, 0.16 V hysteresis typical, 4.0..4.32 V rising), and the module runs down to
  ~3.2 V at the bank (the buck in 100 % mode; the S3 needs 3.0 V). **~1.0 V x 4.4 mF = 4.4 mC: about 150 ms of boot at
  30 mA** (the boot's current and length are *unmeasured*: the study's "a few mF for a slow start").
- **The listening windows:** 0.6..0.8 mC each (the study's estimate) sag the bank only **0.15..0.18 V**.
- **Charging from a 5 V output:** both pins together give ~5..7 mA into an empty bank: **~3 s** to the start threshold.
- **Why these parts:** no supercap is placeable at JLC; MLCCs lose most of their capacitance at 4 V bias (dozens
  needed); tantalum is ~10x the price. Two 10 x 10 mm cans store the most per board area of the SMD aluminium parts
  JLC stocks (1000 uF 8 x 10: 88 mm²/mF of footprint; 2200 uF 10 x 10: 65 mm²/mF).
- **Leakage** (*unverified*, the one figure that matters most here): the datasheet's limit, 0.01 CV, is ~139 uA a can
  at rated voltage after 2 minutes; aluminium caps at 70 % of rating after hours typically leak a few percent of that.
  **Measure it**: it adds directly to the 3 mA budget.
- 6.3 V rating: the rail never exceeds VBUS (5.25 V, 5.5 V from a careless charger) minus a Schottky.

### 3.3 V: the TPS62842 buck (chosen) against an LDO

| | TPS62842DGRR (C2873354) + 2.2 uH | RT9080-33 LDO (C841192) | XC6206P332 LDO (C5446, Basic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiescent current | **60 nA** (120 nA in 100 % mode) | 2 uA | 1 uA |
| Input current for 2.5 mA out at 4.4 V (85 % efficient, *the efficiency at this load unverified*) | **~2.2 mA** | 2.5 mA | 2.5 mA |
| Peak current (Wi-Fi TX in stick mode, ~350 mA) | 750 mA | 600 mA | **200 mA: too weak** |
| Price (snapshot) | $0.40 + $0.11 inductor, 2 Extended lines | $0.12, 1 Extended line | $0.13 Basic |

The buck saves ~0.3 mA of the ~2.5 mA the study budgets (12 %; more as the bank rises) for ~$0.40 a board and one
more Extended line: on a 5 mA source that is the margin. R7 = **51 k** sets 3.3 V (TI table 1: 50.21..54.39 k; 51 k is
a Basic part, 52.3 k is not). MODE low: power-save (PFM) mode.

### Start-up and holding the buck on (BUCK_EN)

BUCK_EN = the detector **or** VBUS **or** PWR_HOLD (GPIO4), through D4 / D5 (BAV70, nanoamp leakage). C4 (100 nF) and
R6 (10 M) keep it high **~1 s after** the detector drops (the buck's EN pull-down is off once it runs, TI datasheet
8.3): time for the firmware to raise PWR_HOLD. **The firmware must set PWR_HOLD high early (a bootloader hook if the
boot is slow) and keep it held in light and deep sleep** (an RTC GPIO: `rtc_gpio_hold_en`); releasing it below 4.0 V
switches everything off until the bank is back above ~4.16 V. A brown-out during the boot cannot loop: the timer ends,
the bank recharges, it starts again. With USB, VBUS holds BUCK_EN.

### Static draw, everything off (the bank charging, *datasheet typicals*)

Detector 0.5 uA, the VSYS divider 2.4 uA at 4.8 V, the buck's shutdown 25 nA, R6 0.4 uA while BUCK_EN is high, the
diodes' leakage: **~3 uA plus the cans' leakage**.

## Pin map (for the firmware's board.h)

| Function | GPIO | Module pin | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MIDI_TX** | **21** | 25 | UART TX (any UART by the GPIO matrix), idle high; through R15 10 ohm to the MIDI OUT tip. An RTC GPIO: the ULP can send the ticks. Not strapping |
| **LED** | **38** | 34 | active high, R9 5.1 k: ~0.27 mA lit (red, 1.9 V) |
| **BOOT / user button** | **0** | 4 | to GND; held at reset = download mode. Internal pull-up |
| **VBANK_ADC** | **1** | 5 | ADC1_CH0: VSYS / 2 (1 M / 1 M, 100 nF); 11 dB attenuation reads to ~3.1 V = 6.2 V at the bank |
| **VBUS_SENSE** | **2** | 6 | ADC1_CH1 or digital: VBUS / 2 (100 k / 100 k) = 2.5 V when USB is plugged |
| **PWR_HOLD** | **4** | 8 | high keeps the buck on; hold it through sleep (RTC GPIO) |
| USB D- / D+ | 19 / 20 | 23 / 24 | the native USB (stick mode) |
| EN | | 45 | RC reset (R8 10 k, C7 1 uF); no reset button |
| MIDI OUT ring | | | +3V3 through R14 33 ohm (0805) |

Not used, left open: everything else, including the strapping pins GPIO3, 45, 46 and the console UART (GPIO43 / 44).

## Layout (tiny, 27.0 x 36.0 mm, 2 layers, 1.6 mm)

- **Top:** the module (antenna at the bottom edge, its end 0.3 mm inside it), the USB-C on the top edge (right of
  centre, so its four shell legs miss the jacks below), the buck block top left, the button and the LED down the left,
  the POWER IN diodes down the right, the cable pads (P2: left edge, P3: right edge).
- **Bottom:** **MIDI OUT (J2) on the left edge near the top, POWER IN (J3) on the right edge 7.7 mm lower** (staggered:
  the board is narrower than two jacks), **C1 under the left half, C2 under the module's right half**, the small parts
  under the module. **Nothing under the antenna:** a keep-out (no copper, track, via or pour on either layer) from
  0.3 mm above the antenna's start to the bottom edge, the full width.
- The jacks' peg holes go through the board; the top parts are kept clear of them.
- **Cable instead of a jack:** P2 / P3, three pads each (T, R, S, 1.5 x 2.0 mm, top side) beside each jack's edge: solder
  a ~5 cm cable with a DIN plug there and leave the jack unfitted (remove it from the BOM line before ordering).
- Routing: `route.py`, 0.2 mm signal tracks, 0.4..0.5 mm power, 0.15 mm clearance, vias 0.6 / 0.3 mm; ground poured on
  both layers and stitched.

## Assembly at JLCPCB

`make_assembly.py` writes `bom.csv` and `cpl.csv`. Every footprint is **JLC's own** (EasyEDA), so Mid X / Mid Y are
the footprint origin and the rotation is KiCad's: no rotation table. The script places JLC's footprint for each LCSC
code at the CPL position and checks every pad against the board's (all within 0.01 mm). **Bottom parts:** the rotation
is KiCad's as seen from the top (what KiCad's own position export writes); how JLC reads a bottom rotation is
*unverified*: **check every bottom part in JLC's placement preview before paying**, the jacks and C1 / C2 (polarity)
first.

## Case (`case/`)

`tiny_case.scad`: a deep **base** (the cans: 11 mm under the board) with the two jack holes in its side walls, a shallow
**lid** with the USB-C opening in its top wall, a hole for the **button cap** (printed: a 3.0 mm stem through the lid, a
flange under it) and the **LED window** (1.8 mm hole). They meet at the board's top face with a rabbet; three posts in
each clamp the board at its three free corners. No screws: a drop of glue or tape (*unverified*: the rabbet's fit).
`make_stl.sh` exports the STLs, checks that neither half nor the cap meets the board's mockup, and that every STL is one
closed solid. Outer size: see "Status".

## Unverified, all in one place

- **The cans' leakage** and the boot's charge (current x time): both decide the reservoir; measure them.
- The buck's efficiency at 1..5 mA from 4..4.7 V (TI's curves were not read for these points).
- **The jack's pins** (PJ-320D-B-SMT): tip = pad 4, ring = pad 3, sleeve = pad 1, pad 2 open (hardware/pcb's reading of
  XKB's drawing, matching JLC's footprint); check with a meter. Its peg holes' fit, and the USB-C's shell legs 0.17 mm
  from J3's tab (physically).
- **The electrolytics' polarity**: pad 1 = + (EasyEDA's convention, the chamfered corners); check in JLC's preview.
- **Bottom-side rotations in the CPL** (above).
- **The antenna:** the keep-out follows Espressif's rule of no copper under the antenna, but C2's metal can sits on the
  other side 0.6 mm from the antenna's start, and the board is small; RX only near the sender should be fine, not
  measured.
- R5's pulse rating; the hosts' tolerance of the 1 A inrush.
- The MIDI 3.3 V drive into real gear (CA-033 values); a mono plug in the MIDI OUT jack shorts the ring: 100 mA through
  the 33 ohm 0805 (0.33 W into a 0.125 W part) from USB power: do not.
- **Swapping the jacks** (MIDI OUT into a gear's MIDI OUT) pushes up to ~7 mA back into +3V3, which the buck cannot
  sink: the labels OUT / PWR on the board and the case matter.
- A ground loop: in stick mode with POWER IN also plugged, the gear's ground meets the computer's (MIDI avoids that
  with the receiver's opto); unplug one.
- All heights (the mockup), the USB-C's axis height (1.63 mm), the board's 1.6 mm.
- JLC's fees other than those seen in earlier quotes (see `fab/cost.md`), and the stock on the day of ordering (the
  jack: 523 at the snapshot, 2 a board).
