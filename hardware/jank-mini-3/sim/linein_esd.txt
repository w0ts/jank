-3 dB band (mono): 3.6 Hz .. 13.3 kHz; mid-band gain A-B / v = 0.544 (node A alone 0.544)
clipping at 1 kHz: mono 2.64 V peak (+7.6 dBu); one channel only 5.27 V peak. Line level (+4 dBu = 1.74 V peak) fits mono with +3.6 dB to spare
ground-offset rejection with 1 % R, 5 % C (Monte Carlo 4000): >= 36 dB at 50 Hz, >= 35 dB at 1 kHz
fault +12 V step on the tip: node would reach +4.73 V, held at +3.80 V by the pin's diodes, ~124 uA into the pin for ~20 ms (PESD15VL2BT off below 17 V)
fault -12 V step on the tip: node would reach -1.80 V, held at -0.50 V by the pin's diodes, ~173 uA into the pin for ~20 ms (PESD15VL2BT off below 17 V)
plug detect: no plug -> J7's ring switch (3) on the ring (2), R37 100k down vs R38 1M up: LIN_DET = 0.30 V (low); a plug opens it: 3.3 V through 1M (high)

## Buttons
- UP (IO16) / DOWN (IO21): the LCD-2's 4.7k pull-ups (R4 / R5): pressed 0.70 mA, released 3.3 V; NO (IO6) / YES (IO18): the S3's internal pull-up (~45 k, ESP32-S3 datasheet): pressed ~73 uA. No RC fitted (C80..C83 DNP pads): the firmware debounces. A button shorts only its GPIO to GND: a GPIO driven high by a firmware fault into a pressed button is limited by the S3's drive (~40 mA, drive strength 2), a fault, not a damage case at the default drive.
- ESD: the TS-1187A's actuator is plastic and its contacts sit inside; the case covers the frame. No exposed metal on the button nets, so no ESD part (the connectors below are the exposed paths).
